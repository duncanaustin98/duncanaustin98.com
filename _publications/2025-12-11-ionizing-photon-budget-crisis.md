A study of the ionizing properties of 1,721 galaxies at 5.6 < z < 6.5 using deep JWST/NIRCam imaging from the NEP, JADES, and PRIMER surveys, deriving HII clumping factor constraints that help resolve the tension between the ionizing photon budget and the observed reionization history. Accepted for publication in The Astrophysical Journal; not yet assigned a DOI, so linked via arXiv.

*This is one of my first-author papers.*
