I'm a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics](https://www.jodrellbank.manchester.ac.uk/), University of Manchester, working with Professor Christopher Conselice. I use imaging and spectroscopy from the *James Webb Space Telescope* to find and study galaxies in the early Universe, from the first few hundred million years after the Big Bang through the Epoch of Reionization. My PhD thesis, [*Inferring the properties of star forming galaxies in the Epoch of Reionization with JWST*](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/inferring-the-properties-of-star-forming-galaxies-in-the-epoch-of/), covers much of this work in more depth.

Most of my work sits within the [EPOCHS survey](https://jwst-epochs.github.io/), drawing on deep NIRCam and NIRSpec data from PEARLS, JADES, and other public JWST fields. I use these to build large samples of high-redshift galaxies and study their sizes, morphologies, star-forming properties, and role in reionizing the intergalactic medium. My first-author work includes the discovery of a large population of faint galaxies at 8 < z < 16 in the NGDEEP survey, then one of the deepest JWST fields ever observed.

Have a look at my [publications](/publications/) for the full list, or my [blog](/year-archive/), where I write in a bit more depth about the physics behind reionization and high-redshift galaxy formation.
