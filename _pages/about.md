I'm a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the [Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics](https://www.jodrellbank.manchester.ac.uk/), University of Manchester, working with Professor Christopher Conselice. My research uses imaging and spectroscopy from the *James Webb Space Telescope* to find and characterise galaxies in the early Universe, from the first few hundred million years after the Big Bang through the Epoch of Reionization.

I'm part of the [EPOCHS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13742) collaboration and work extensively with data from the JWST PEARLS and JADES surveys, using deep NIRCam and NIRSpec observations to build samples of high-redshift galaxies and study their sizes, morphologies, star-forming properties, and role in reionizing the intergalactic medium. My first-author work includes the discovery of a large population of faint galaxies at 8 < z < 16 in the NGDEEP survey, one of the deepest JWST fields observed to date.

Have a look at my [publications](/publications/) for the full list, or my [blog](/year-archive/) where I write about the physics behind reionization and high-redshift galaxy formation in a bit more depth.
